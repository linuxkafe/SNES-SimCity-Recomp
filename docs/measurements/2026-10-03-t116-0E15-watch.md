## 1. Peer — $0E15 writes from bank $03 (`$03:8222` = `STZ $0E15`)

> ### ⚠️ CORRECTED by T117 (`ab73931`) — the BYTES and the COUNT in this section were wrong
>
> **R-048.** This document wrote: *"the 3-byte `STZ $0E15` at `$03:8222`"* and *"ROM byte
> `64 15`"*. **There is no `64` anywhere in `$03:821D`–`$03:8242`.** The ROM bytes at file
> offset `0x18222` are **`9C 15 0E 9C`**. `0x64` is `STZ dp` (2 bytes); `0x9C` is
> **`STZ abs` (3 bytes)** — authoritative from this repository's own declarative table,
> `snesrecomp/recompiler/snes65816.py:369`, `(0x9C,'STZ',ABS,3)`.
>
> **R-050.** *"56 writes"* is **56 bus writes = 28 executions.** With `M=0` the `STZ` is a
> 16-bit store, so each execution logs **two** lines (`$0E15` then `$0E16`). There are
> **28 distinct frames**, always exactly 2 lines each.
>
> **What SURVIVES, and is the whole point of this correction:** the **mnemonic**
> `STZ $0E15` was right. `STZ $0E15` at `$03:8222` **does** execute in the peer — **28
> times, f3068–f8881** — and **never** in ours (0 bank-`$03` writes to `$0E15` in 9 000
> frames, tier-independently). **The divergence is real and located.** Only the byte, the
> implied length and the count were wrong.
>
> Full evidence: `docs/measurements/2026-10-03-t117-8222-disassembly.md`.
