# Measurement — C-041 answered, and the bank-03 boundary re-measured (2026-10-02)

**The question.** Claim C-041, open since the whole-run histogram was taken:
**is `$03:8026` (`INC.w $0B51`, ROM `0x18026`) among the bank-03 PCs that
execute?** The instrument that produced the 921 could not answer it: the dump
prints the top 60 PCs by host-ms, and the entry said so itself
(`docs/measurements/2026-10-02-deck-interp-histogram.md` §5.2).

**The answer: NO. Zero.** `$03:8026` executes **0 times** — not in the
interpreter, not as an AOT block, on either machine, over frames 0–3700. And the
stronger form: **every bank-03 PC that executes lies in `$03C63D`–`$03E57E`.**
The whole `$038000`–`$03C63C` region, which contains the tick, executes **no
instruction at all** on either tier.

**What this does and does not settle.** It settles C-041, and it moves C-008
from *OPEN* to *MEASURED* with an instrument that can see both tiers — which is
exactly the move that was refused before, because the earlier version of it
rested on a premise (`$0012 == 0`) that measurement refuted. It does **not**
name a cause. "$03:8026 never runs" is a location, not a mechanism, and the
belief that the city tick lives there is still **[INFERRED]** from the reference
implementation's trace.

**Two machines, and an environment-fidelity caveat that applies to one of them.**
Deck `steamdeck`, gcc 15.1.1, cmake 4.0.3, Zen 2, 8 threads, compiling natively
against a hand-assembled glibc header prefix at `/home/deck/sysroot` via
`-idirafter` because 503 of the 504 `/usr/include` paths pacman claims are absent
from disk — **every Deck number on this page inherits that caveat.** Dev host
`seyon`, i5-8500T, 6 threads, Ubuntu 24.04, gcc 13.3.0. Runs are headless with
`SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy` and `SDL_AUDIODRIVER=dummy`, and all of them drive
`scripts/d_city.script`, the script that reaches a live city.

## 1. The instrument change

Two dev-only knobs behind the existing `-DSNESRECOMP_INTERP_PROFILE`, in
`snesrecomp/runner/src/snes/interp_bridge.c` (submodule
`linuxkafe/snesrecomp`, branch `instrument/bank03-pc-dump`, commit `eb7347a`;
no shipped configuration defines the macro, so production builds are unchanged):

| variable | what it prints |
|---|---|
| `SNESRECOMP_INTERP_DUMP_BANK=03` | at exit, **every** distinct PC in that bank with its step count, ascending by PC |
| `SNESRECOMP_INTERP_TRACE_FRAMES=lo-hi` | every interpreted PC executed in that host-frame window, **all banks**, in execution order |

`SNESRECOMP_INTERP_DUMP_BANK` is the answer to C-041. `…TRACE_FRAMES` is the
answer to "what is the last thing bank 03 executes, and where does control go",
which needs the final instruction **and its successor** in the same log.

**Falsification before use.** The dump must agree with the instrument it extends,
or it is a second unverified number. On the boot window (f0–200) it reproduced
the previous session's Deck row **exactly**, and the per-bank step sums equal the
`[coverage]` totals:

**Cross-machine reproducibility, to the unit.** The host's plain run reproduced
the Deck's *entire* whole-run histogram, every bank, including the spin-heavy
step counts — `$00` 1822 / 26 856 340, `$01` 1814 / 5 730 728, `$02` 542 /
601 366, `$03` 921 / 515 043, `$05` 956 / 1 510 936.

**But step counts are not invariant run to run.** A third host run of the same
binary with an unrelated no-op environment variable set gave `$00` **28 769 361**
and `$01` **5 975 370** steps against 26 856 340 and 5 730 728 — a 7% spread in
the spinlock-dominated banks. **Distinct-PC counts never moved**: 1822 / 1814 /
542 / 921 / 956 in every run on both machines. The honest reading is that the
number of times the guest spins is wall-clock dependent and the set of addresses
it reaches is not; the 3rd instrument trap's sibling is "a step count is not a
deterministic quantity, a distinct-PC count is".

## 3. The boundary is f3270/f3271, not f3301 — C-039 corrected

The old boundary came from 100-frame brackets, which can only say "the
f3100–3300 bracket is non-empty". A per-frame stream says more:

```
$ grep "^\[itb\]" run.log | grep 'pc=\$03' | sed 's/.*f=\([0-9]*\).*/\1/' \
    | sort -n | uniq -c | tail -8
   12861 3264
   12860 3265
   12861 3266
   12860 3267
   12859 3268
   12861 3269
    7341 3270
      16 3271
```

**Frame-for-frame identical on the Deck and on the host**, including the two
partial frames. Bank 03 execution is **bursty** — nothing at all in f3243–f3258,
12 861 steps/frame in f3259–f3269, a partial frame, then **16 steps in f3271 and
never again** through the end of the trace window.

So **"bank 03 executes up to and including f3300" is REFUTED as stated.** The
true statement is narrower and frame-resolved: **the last frame containing any
bank-03 execution is f3271**, and there is none in f3272–f3700. The 78-frame gap
to the city's arrival at ≈f3378 becomes **≈107 frames**. It remains a
correlation, recorded as one.

## 4. What bank 03 executes last — decoded from ROM bytes, not from a disassembler

The tail of f3271, verbatim from both machines:

```
[itb] f=3271 pc=$009313    [itb] f=3271 pc=$03D2B5
[itb] f=3271 pc=$009315    [itb] f=3271 pc=$03D2B6
[itb] f=3271 pc=$009317    [itb] f=3271 pc=$03D2B7
[itb] f=3271 pc=$008221    [itb] f=3271 pc=$00805A
[itb] f=3271 pc=$008222    [itb] f=3271 pc=$00804D
[itb] f=3271 pc=$03D299    [itb] f=3271 pc=$00804F
       … 13 more bank-03 PCs …   [itb] f=3271 pc=$00805C
                              [itb] f=3271 pc=$008061
                              [itb] f=3271 pc=$008288 …
```

The auto-generated disassembly has been caught inventing an instruction in this
project before, so every byte below is read out of the ROM and every claimed
length is checked against the PC sequence:

```
$ python3 -c "d=open('SimCity (USA).sfc','rb').read(); \
    print(d[3*0x8000+0x5299:3*0x8000+0x52B8].hex(' '))"
a5 14 10 ea e2 20 a5 b3 29 7f 85 b1 c2 20 a9 01 00 85 12 e2 20
a9 ff 8d 2a 0b a9 00 48 ab 6b
```

| PC | bytes | instruction | next PC in the log? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `$03D299` | `A5 14` | `LDA $14` | `$03D29B` ✓ |
| `$03D29B` | `10 EA` | `BPL` (not taken) | `$03D29D` ✓ |
| `$03D29D` | `E2 20` | `SEP #$20` | `$03D29F` ✓ |
| `$03D29F` | `A5 B3` | `LDA $B3` | `$03D2A1` ✓ |
| `$03D2A1` | `29 7F` | `AND #$7F` | `$03D2A3` ✓ |
| `$03D2A3` | `85 B1` | `STA $B1` | `$03D2A5` ✓ |
| `$03D2A5` | `C2 20` | `REP #$20` | `$03D2A7` ✓ |
| `$03D2A7` | `A9 01 00` | `LDA #$0001` | `$03D2AA` ✓ |
| `$03D2AA` | `85 12` | **`STA $12`** | `$03D2AC` ✓ |
| `$03D2AC` | `E2 20` | `SEP #$20` | `$03D2AE` ✓ |
| `$03D2AE` | `A9 FF` | `LDA #$FF` | `$03D2B0` ✓ |
| `$03D2B0` | `8D 2A 0B` | `STA $0B2A` | `$03D2B3` ✓ |
| `$03D2B3` | `A9 00` | `LDA #$00` | `$03D2B5` ✓ |
| `$03D2B5` | `48` | `PHA` | `$03D2B6` ✓ |
| `$03D2B6` | `AB` | `PLB` | `$03D2B7` ✓ |
| **`$03D2B7`** | **`6B`** | **`RTL`** | **`$00805A`** ✓ |

**16 instructions, 16 predicted next-PCs, 16 matches.** The decode is verified
against the byte stream, not read off a listing.

**So the answer to "call, return, or jump out?" is: a return.** The last bank-03
instruction is an `RTL`, and it returns into `$00:805A`, whose bytes are `80 F1`
— `BRA $804D`. The trace then shows `$804D` (`A5 12` = `LDA $12`), `$804F`
(`D0 0B` = `BNE $805C`, **taken**), `$805C` (`20 61 80` = `JSR $8061`), `$8061`
(`20 88 82` = `JSR $8288`). Each of those four lengths is confirmed by the next
logged PC, so the whole chain is byte-checked.

**The entry, and one thing that is INFERRED.** Immediately before the bank-03
entry the log shows `$00:8221` = `AB` (`PLB`) and `$00:8222` = `40 0D 93`
(`RTI`). An `RTI` does not branch; the next PC comes from the stack, so the
`$03D299` that follows was **dispatched by a return-from-interrupt**, i.e. a task
stack, not called by the instruction before it. That is **[INFERRED]** — it
follows from `RTI` semantics and is consistent with the neighbouring bytes
(`$00:821E` = `FC 23 82` is `JSR ($8223,X)`, a dispatch-table call, followed by
`PLB; RTI`), but no instrument here dumped the stack, so it is not measured.

**And note what the sequence does to `$12`.** `STA $12` at `$03D2AA` is in the
last bank-03 execution of the run. The bank-00 loop that receives the return
tests `$12` at `$00:804D` and takes the `BNE` to `$805C`. That is the *third*
time this project has produced a value that refutes the "`$0012` is the gate"
theory (ledger R-005…R-008) — this time from an execution trace rather than from
a WRAM sample, and still **not** a cause.

## 5. The AOT tier, measured over a whole run for the first time

**This closes a documented blind spot.** The previous page said: *"There is no
whole-run AOT histogram, so 'bank 03 executes no AOT blocks anywhere' is NOT
measured."* It is now.

**And the first attempt produced nothing at all, which is a fourth instrument
trap.** `SNESRECOMP_AOTBLK=0-3700` over 4 000 frames logged **zero** `[aotblk]`
lines and printed no warning:

```
$ grep -c aotblk /tmp/i1/aot.log
0
```

Cause, measured: `cpu_trace_block()` is a **no-op** unless `SNESRECOMP_TRACE=1`
(`snesrecomp/runner/src/cpu_trace.h`, `#else` branch). Every AOT-side instrument
in the framework — `AOTBLK`, the `CYC_WATCH` complement, `mx_async` — lives
behind that define, and **no CMake option in this repository builds it**: with
`TRACE=1` the link fails on 24 undefined references into `debug_server.c`. So
until this change those knobs accepted their variable and did nothing.
`CMakeLists.txt` now has `-DSNESRECOMP_TRACE_BUILD=OFF` (default off) which adds
`debug_server.c` and links pthreads, which is what makes the tier buildable at
all.

With the tier built (host, `-DSNESRECOMP_TRACE=1`, 4 000 frames, 4 m 33 s):

| tier | window | total block entries | `$00` | `$01` | `$03` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AOT | f0–f3700 | **1 430 539** | 1 100 383 | 330 138 | **18** |

The 18 bank-03 AOT entries are **all in f3270**, at `$03B477`, `$03B491` (×7),
`$03B49B`, `$03B4A0` (×4), `$03B4A9`. Per-100-frame buckets:

```
f3200  bank $00  15783      f3300  bank $00   6670
f3200  bank $03     18      f3300  bank $01  29866
f3400  bank $00   3200      f3500  bank $00   3200
f3400  bank $01  99898      f3500  bank $01  99681
f3600  bank $00   3200      f3700  bank $00     32
f3600  bank $01  99681      f3700  bank $01   1012
```

**Two things follow, and only two.**

1. **Bank 03's AOT and interpreted activity die in the same place** — f3270 and
   f3271, adjacent frames. Bank 03's silence is not an artefact of one tier.
2. **In the live-city window the AOT side is banks 00 and 01 as well** (f3400–f3700:
   3 200 + 99 681 per 100 frames, `$03` **0**), so "the city simulates by some
   other bank-03 path" is now excluded on both tiers rather than on one.
   Banks 02/04/05/06/07: **zero** AOT entries in the whole run.

**The Deck's AOT histogram is still missing.** The Deck build of the trace tier
was launched after this page was written; if it is absent from
`docs/measurements/` afterwards, the AOT numbers here are **host-only**.

## 6. What this does NOT establish

- **Why** bank 03 stops. Not measured. `$03D2B7` is an `RTL` that returns to a
  bank-00 loop that then keeps running; nothing here says who was supposed to
  call that task again, or whether the caller exists at all.
- **That the city tick is at `$03:8026`.** Still **[INFERRED]** from the peer.
  This page measures that a location does not execute; it does not measure that
  the location is the right one. The peer's `$0B51` climbs to `006D`, and the
  question this raises — *what the peer does instead* — is untouched by
  anything here.
- **That `$0B12` or `$0B14` gates anything** (ledger R-017, R-018).
- Anything after f3700, beyond the settle protocol's 9 000-frame snapshots.
- **That the step counts are reproducible.** See §2: they are not, in the
  spin-heavy banks.
