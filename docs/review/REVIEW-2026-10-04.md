## The BLOCKER: filed, and DOWNGRADED — with the reason recorded

Cinico filed the BLOCKER as: *"`RE_CITY_FREEZE.md` index row 43 claims `f3301` is
CURRENT while the maintained summary says `f3271`"* (E-04).

**The finding is correct and the evidence is exact.** Index row 43 marked the bank-`$03`
stop boundary `f3301` / `CURRENT` while the maintained summary at lines 15–19 of the
**same file** states `f3271` and records `f3301` as a retracted 100-frame bracket.

**But it is not a BLOCKER, and downgrading it is the honest call.** The rubric reserves
BLOCKER for a finding where *"the project's stated purpose is defeated, or a published
claim is false"* — including *"a retracted cause presented as fact."* An **index row**
that lags its own banner is a stale cross-reference inside a document, not a retracted
cause presented as fact in a claim. **Purista and Utilizador independently filed the same
defect as MAJOR.** The moderator aligns with them.

**This is the second time in this project's history that three lenses found one thing
the author had not seen** — and again it was not a measurement error. It was a
maintained index that had stopped tracking the body it claims authority over.

**It was also actively harmful, not merely untidy:** at the moment it was found I had a
measurement session running against that file. Every agent that opens
`RE_CITY_FREEZE.md` reads the index as authoritative. **Corrected in `58392bd`** — row 43
now reads `SUPERSEDED`, names entry **(t)** as the authority, and keeps the original
claim visible rather than deleting it.

## The finding I acted on fastest: a MINOR that outranked the BLOCKER

Cinico filed as **MINOR**: *"The peer's claimed `STZ $0E15` at `$03:8222` does not decode
to `STZ $0E15` in the ROM."*

**MINOR. And it was more important than the BLOCKER.** I verified it directly:

```
file offset 0x18222  ->  9c 15 0e 9c
```

`0x9C` is **`STZ $sr,S`** — *stack-relative* — not `0x64` (`STZ dp`). T116 had written
*"ROM byte `64 15`"* into its own measurement document, and the README repeated it.

**The damage is precisely bounded, and this distinction is the whole point:**

| | status |
|---|---|
| peer writes `$0E15`, 28 distinct frames, 56 writes, f3068–f8881, control fired | **[MEASURED] — stands** |
| our build never writes `$0E15`; bank `$03` silent from f3271 | **[MEASURED] — stands** |
| **those writes come from `STZ $0E15` at `$03:8222`** | **REFUTED — the byte is `9C`** |

This is the shape of **R-040**: *the measurement reproduces to the unit, the attribution
was an inference.* **I reported T116 as a "confirmation" when it was a correct observation
carrying an incorrect explanation, and I did not check the byte.** A MINOR finding
outranked a BLOCKER because severity and importance are not the same axis, and a rubric
that only counts severities will keep missing this.

**A second correction fell out of it:** the write-watch logged 56 writes, but only **28
distinct frames** (2 writes per frame). I reported "56 times"; the frame count is 28.
